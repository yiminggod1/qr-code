QR code best practices

Keep contrast high

Dark modules on a light background are generally easier for scanners to detect.

Keep the quiet zone

Leave clear space around the QR code so its edges remain easy to detect.

Test before printing

Scan the exact final image or print at the intended size before distributing it.

QR Code Generator FAQ

Is this QR code generator free?

Yes. Basic QR code creation and PNG download are free.

Do I need an account?

No. You can create a basic QR code without signing up.

Does the QR code expire?

A static QR code itself has no expiration date. A URL can stop working, so test important codes regularly.

Can I use QR codes for business?

Yes. QR codes can be used on menus, packaging, posters, business cards, events, signage, and marketing materials.

Is my information uploaded?

The basic generator builds the QR content in your browser. Avoid entering information you do not want encoded into the QR code.

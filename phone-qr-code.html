Where to use a phone QR code

Use a phone QR code on business cards, storefront signs, vehicle graphics, brochures, invoices, service counters, and event materials.

Best practices

  • Use a complete international phone number when appropriate.
  • Print at a size that scans easily from the expected distance.
  • Test the finished design before distributing it.

How to create a URL QR code

  1. Paste your website address.
  2. Generate the code.
  3. Scan-test it on your phone.
  4. Download the PNG.

Where to use URL QR codes

Use them on menus, posters, brochures, product packaging, business cards, event signs, classroom materials, and storefront displays.

Printing tips

Keep strong contrast, leave clear space around the code, and test the final printed size before distribution.
